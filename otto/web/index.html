For the person who runs the whole IT function alone. Why this exists.

EYES ON EVERYTHING
THAT RUNS WITHOUT YOU

A daemon owns the state, this dashboard shows it, an otto CLI drives it, and one persona fronts all of it. Otto does not replace the skills and commands in ~/.claude; it makes them visible, schedulable, and accountable.

01An alert is not a gap. An alert is something that broke while Otto was watching it. A gap is something nothing was ever watching. A stale schedule is the first; a schedule with no staleness alarm is the second, and that is the class of problem you cannot notice by looking harder.
02"Otto broke" never looks like "the work failed". Every run carries two lanes: OTTO (spawned, ran, reported) and WORK (what the session concluded). A 529 from upstream and a session that genuinely could not do the task are both red in the naive reading, and that reading has cost real money.
03Silence sends, and the clock is visible. A message Otto wants to send a colleague is held, shown in full with its reason, and goes out only if you say nothing. The countdown is the loudest thing on the card.
04Every click teaches the CLI. The bar at the bottom shows the otto command your last click was worth. Copy it. The dashboard is a way in, not the only door.
